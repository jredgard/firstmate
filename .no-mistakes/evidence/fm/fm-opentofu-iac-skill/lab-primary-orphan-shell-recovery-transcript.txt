● Captain, short answer: no, not by hand and not yet. I loaded the opentofu-iac-management skill, and its "Failed-create recovery" section plus the findings ledger govern this exactly.

  When the shell may be removed (all conditions together, per IAC-009):

  - It is a release-owned, failed, revisionless, stateless Container App shell with no healthy revision.
  - Pre-apply receipts prove it was absent before that exact release-owned create, so provenance is established, not inferred.
  - Live type, name, scope, provisioning status and revisions have been reconciled against that run and the state file.
  - Removal happens under the same exclusive release lock and an explicitly authorized recovery contract, with the deletion verified and a redacted recovery receipt retained.
  - Before touching anything, the Azure refusal itself is classified from stage-level evidence as a provisioning failure rather than admission, authorization, image-pull or identity-resolution trouble
    (IAC-014, IAC-004, IAC-008).

  What must be refused:

  - Any shell that is pre-existing, of unknown provenance, has a healthy revision, is stateful, or belongs to an unrelated successful resource.
  - Recovery being stretched into generic import, adoption, taint or deletion authority. An ad hoc az delete is exactly the hand-delete of an inconvenient collision that IAC-009 names as the
    anti-pattern.
  - If ownership or recovery authority cannot be proved, the rule is stop and escalate. The skill grants no infrastructure mutation or recovery authority on its own.

  The previously approved plan is dead afterwards. A failed apply is never success, and changed live inventory or state requires a fresh guarded saved plan with its own approval (IAC-011 and the
  orchestration step 4 rule). You cannot edit or reuse the old approved plan, and mock-green or zero-drift evidence cannot stand in for the new gate (IAC-012).

  Net consequence for the QA rerun: the only compliant path is receipted reconciliation and a locked, authorized cleanup of that one shell, then a new plan and approval. Nothing here was run or
  touched.
