=== background-gerrit
when reattaching, use `no-mistakes axi run` with `--skip push,pr,ci` and a bounded `--wait`, backgrounded the same way
=== foreground-gerrit
when reattaching, use `no-mistakes axi run` with `--skip push,pr,ci` and a bounded `--wait` and issue the same foreground call again, one at a time, until a gate or outcome comes back
=== foreground-gerrit-skip
when reattaching, use `no-mistakes axi run` with `--skip push,pr,ci,lint` and a bounded `--wait` and issue the same foreground call again, one at a time, until a gate or outcome comes back
